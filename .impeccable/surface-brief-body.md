# Surface brief: home (Al-Nour Auto showroom)

Scope: single-page Persuade surface, bilingual AR/EN, dark theme only. Audience: Algerian car buyers who need price, financing, and a WhatsApp route to a human. Job: qualify a lead. Action: submit name, wilaya, phone via pre-filled wa.me message. Proof: transparent Mourabaha math, indicative DZD prices, 58-wilaya coverage. Constraint: no fabricated logos, testimonials, or contact claims.

## Direction contract

THESIS: the page sells trust through visible arithmetic, not lifestyle imagery; it refuses the category default of a full-bleed car photo hero with centered headline and three equal feature cards.

OWN-WORLD: zinc-950 ground with zinc-900 panels, one gold accent (#f5b942) used only on actions, key numbers, and focus rings; Archivo (Latin) + Cairo (Arabic) display sans, Geist Mono for measurements and prices; uniform 6px radius, hairline zinc-800 rules instead of card stacks; a procedural line-art car drawing system and one WebGL studio as the only illustration language.

STORY: visitor sees six Chinese brands and real prices, moves their own down payment and term and watches the monthly payment move, then sends a complete, pre-qualified message to the showroom on WhatsApp.

FIRST VIEWPORT: nav bar (monogram, four links, AR/EN toggle, gold quote CTA) over an asymmetric hero; headline and 17-word subtext anchored left in a 5-column measure, a cursor-tracked Three.js studio of a procedural car occupying the right 7 columns and bleeding to the edge, two CTAs (gold primary "Request a quote", ghost secondary "Browse stock") visible without scroll; mobile stacks copy over a scrimmed, lowered car.

FORM: asymmetric split hero (position 1 of the ordered list, seed key: brief-pinned, no roll: user pinned zinc-950 + amber + editorial sans).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
